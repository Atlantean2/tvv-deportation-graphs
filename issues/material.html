Section questions (from the Graphs Collection)

  • Q1. Were Biden-era deportations really as high as Trump’s?
    • • Removals (formal orders), FY-based: Trump 1 FY17-20 = 1.20M; Biden FY21-24 = 0.70M (OHSS).
    • • Removals + returns + Title 42 expulsions: Trump 1 = 2.00M; Biden = 4.79M. Title 42 alone = 2.75M under Biden FYs.
    • • Interior removals: Trump 1 FY17-20 = 282K; Biden FY21-24 = 137K. ICE FY2024 = 271,484 total, but only 47,732 ICE-arrested (interior).
    • • Trump 2: ICE 319,980 removals FY2025 (detention stats) vs 442,637 "returned or removed" (budget doc); 356,389 FY2026 to Jul 11. DHS press claims (~900K by May 2026) are not backed by published tables.
    • Numbers and links are in the research notes document and the data CSVs. Sources disagree; see the lean label on every slide.
  • Q2. The definitional dispute: what gets counted as a "deportation"?
    • • Removal = order with re-entry penalties; return = departure without an order; Title 42 expulsion = public-health turn-back (neither).
    • • Obama-era "record deportations" came largely from moving border cases from returns to formal removals (expedited removal, reinstatement). Obama said so himself in 2011.
    • • Refused port-of-entry applicants who withdraw an application are counted as administrative returns, not removals (OHSS, since FY2018). They are in "repatriations" totals, not in removal counts.
    • • So "Biden deported as many as Trump" is true only if returns and/or Title 42 expulsions are counted.
    • Numbers and links are in the research notes document and the data CSVs. Sources disagree; see the lean label on every slide.
  • Q3. Repeat removals, repeat crossings and re-entry
    • • Reinstatements of prior orders (people removed before who came back) = 39% of removals in FY2013 and FY2019 (OHSS).
    • • One-year re-encounter rate at the SW border: about 15% (FY2014-Feb 2020), 45% (Mar-Sep 2020), 35% (FY2021), 26% (FY2022) (OHSS FY2022).
    • • Title 42 drove repeat attempts: no re-entry penalty, so encounter counts overstate unique people (FY2022: 2.34M encounters, 1.74M unique).
    • Numbers and links are in the research notes document and the data CSVs. Sources disagree; see the lean label on every slide.
  • Q4. How many unauthorized immigrants are there?
    • • Mainstream residual estimates: DHS 11.0M (Jan 2022); CMS 11.7M, MPI 13.7M, Pew 14.0M (mid-2023); CIS 15.8M peak (Jan 2025), down to 13.5M (Jul 2026).
    • • High estimates: FAIR 18.6M (2025); Yale/MIT 22.1M (2016, range 16.2-29.5M). CBO flows: net immigration 3.3M in 2023 (Jan 2024 outlook).
    • • Differences come from who counts (parole, TPS, asylum pending), survey (ACS vs CPS), undercount assumptions and gotaway add-ons.
    • Numbers and links are in the research notes document and the data CSVs. Sources disagree; see the lean label on every slide.
  • Q5. Encounters vs apprehensions vs gotaways
    • • Encounters = events (Title 8 apprehensions + inadmissibles + Title 42 expulsions), not unique people.
    • • Known gotaways (seen, not caught): FY2021 387K, FY2022 606K, FY2023 671K (CBP FOIA via X). Unknown gotaways are modeled by DHS, and the estimates are disputed.
    • • FY2026: 98,708 Border Patrol SW apprehensions; 379,243 nationwide encounters (CBP, Oct 16, 2026), the lowest on record.
    • Numbers and links are in the research notes document and the data CSVs. Sources disagree; see the lean label on every slide.
  • Q6. Self-deportation (paid and unpaid)
    • • PAID (CBP Home / Project Homecoming): stipend $1,000 (May 2025), $3,000 holiday offer (Dec 2025), $2,600 (Jan 2026), $3,000 (Sep 2026), plus a free flight. Documented exits: 38,380 (Dec 2025 sworn statement), about 72,000 (Mar 2026, CNN), about 132,000 (Jul 16, 2026, Axios). No official count published; DHS’s Sep 30, 2026 summary was fully redacted in FOIA.
    • • UNPAID, documented: immigration-court voluntary departures 89,494 in Trump 2’s first 16 months vs 11,977 in Biden’s last 16 (Stateline/DDP); 72,761 in FY2026 to Jul 24 (EOIR, combined category). DHS says 82,000+ people with removal or VD orders self-departed, Mar 2025-Sep 14, 2026.
    • • DHS’s 1.6M / 1.9M / 2.2M "self-deported": no DHS method published. The 1.6M came from a CIS estimate of the NET fall in the unauthorized population from the CPS survey, which also counts removals, deaths, status changes and fewer arrivals (PolitiFact: Half True).
    • • Critics: Census (net migration still +1.3M in Jul 2024-Jun 2025; projected +321K by Jul 2026), Brookings/AEI (2025 net migration -295K to -10K), PIIE/Kolko ("almost certainly wrong"), MPI. CIS defends a real but smaller decline (1.1M after the 2026 reweighting; 2.3M by Jul 2026).
    • Numbers and links are in the research notes document and the data CSVs. Sources disagree; see the lean label on every slide.
